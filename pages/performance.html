Personal Adaptation

Your training history becomes
your training model.

SteelSet learns from repeated completed training, including how you respond to progression and training volume. It does not react to one good or bad session. Reliable patterns have to build first.

BOUNDED ADAPTATION

When the evidence is strong enough, Personal Adaptation can refine future targets within controlled limits — without overriding recovery, safety or the core training system.

01
TRAIN Complete real sessions

Load, reps, effort and completed work create the evidence base.

↓
02
LEARN Build your response profile

Repeated history reveals how progression and training volume tend to work for you.

↓
03
VERIFY Wait for reliable patterns

Emerging signals stay separate from established evidence instead of becoming instant prescriptions.

↓
04
ADAPT Refine future targets carefully

Qualified patterns can influence selected targets inside controlled boundaries.
