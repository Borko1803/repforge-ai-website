Common Questions

What happens after my 14-day trial?

You'll be charged the plan rate you selected at signup. If you don't want to continue, cancel before day 14 and you won't be charged anything. No dark patterns, no confusing flows.

Can I switch plans at any time?

Yes — upgrade or downgrade instantly. If you upgrade, you're prorated. If you downgrade, credits roll to your next billing cycle.

Is my data safe?

Your training data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We never sell personal data. You can export or delete everything at any time from your account settings.

Does RepForge AI work for beginners?

Absolutely. The AI assessment detects your training age and programs accordingly. Beginners get simpler, high-frequency programs that build foundational strength before complexity is layered in.

What if I only have home gym equipment?

The program generator supports bodyweight only, dumbbells only, home gym, commercial gym, and custom equipment setups. The AI selects exercises based on what you actually have.

Is there a team or coach plan?

The Elite plan includes coach dashboard access for managing up to 10 athletes. For larger teams or gyms, contact us for custom pricing.
