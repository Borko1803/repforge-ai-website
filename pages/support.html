Contact RepForge AI Support

For general support, account questions or technical problems, contact us at:

REPFORGE AI Support repforgeaisupport@gmail.com

The Gmail link opens a new message in your browser. You can also email the same address from any email service you prefer.

02

Account and sign-in help

Contact Support if you are having trouble with account access, Google Sign-In, account identification or other authenticated RepForge features.

For your protection, we may need to verify enough information to confirm account ownership before helping with account-specific requests.

Do not send passwords, authentication codes or other account credentials to RepForge Support.

03

Workout and training-data questions

If a saved workout, cloud-synchronized session, training plan or other training record appears incorrect, include enough context for us to understand the issue.

Helpful information can include the affected exercise or workout, approximate date, the screen where the issue appears and a short description of what you expected to happen.

RepForge Support can help investigate product behavior, but cannot provide medical diagnosis or individualized medical treatment.

04

Community and safety support

Use the in-app reporting and blocking controls for supported Community safety issues whenever possible.

For a Community concern that cannot be handled through the in-app tools, or for a question about moderation, contact Support.

Community use is governed by the Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.

05

Privacy questions

For questions about personal information, retention, data handling or privacy-related requests, contact:

PRIVACY Privacy Support repforgeaisupport@gmail.com

Read the Privacy Policy for a detailed description of RepForge data handling.

06

Account deletion

RepForge provides permanent account deletion from inside the app.

Settings → Danger Zone → Delete account

If you cannot access the app, use the external Delete Account resource to request deletion.

07

Technical issues

If RepForge crashes, fails to load, cannot complete an action or behaves unexpectedly, contact Support with the details needed to reproduce the problem.

Please avoid sending more personal information than is necessary to explain the issue.

Screenshots can be useful when they show the relevant product state, but review them first and remove unrelated private information where possible.

08

What to include in a support request

A useful support request normally includes:

  • a short description of the problem;
  • the part of RepForge where it happened;
  • what you expected to happen;
  • what actually happened;
  • the approximate date or session involved, when relevant;
  • the email associated with the RepForge account when the request is account-specific;
  • a screenshot when it materially helps explain the issue.

Never send passwords, Google authentication codes, payment-card information or other secret credentials.
