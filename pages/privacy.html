Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2025 · Effective: June 1, 2025

Overview

RepForge AI Inc. ("RepForge," "we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Service.

The short version: We collect your training data, account info, and usage data to power the AI. We don't sell it. You can export or delete it at any time. We use industry-standard encryption.

Data We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and (optionally) a profile photo. If you sign up via Google or GitHub, we receive the data those providers share with us based on your OAuth permissions.

Training & Health Data

The core data you provide to use RepForge includes:

  • Workout logs (exercises, sets, reps, load, RPE, notes)
  • Body measurements (weight, estimated body fat percentage)
  • Sleep quality ratings you manually enter
  • Recovery and readiness notes
  • Goals, training age, and equipment preferences entered during onboarding

Wearable & Third-Party Data

If you connect a wearable device or third-party service (Garmin, Apple Health, WHOOP, Oura), we receive the data you authorize through those integrations, which may include:

  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Resting heart rate
  • Sleep stages and duration
  • Daily step count and activity level
  • Stress scores

We only access the data you explicitly authorize through each integration's consent flow. You can revoke access at any time from your account settings or from the third-party app.

Usage Data

We automatically collect certain information when you use the Service:

  • Device type, operating system, and browser version
  • IP address (anonymized after 30 days)
  • Pages and features you access
  • Session duration and frequency
  • Error logs and crash reports

Payment Information

Payment processing is handled by Stripe. RepForge never stores your full card number. We only receive a payment token and the last 4 digits of your card for display purposes.

How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect to:

  • Power the AI engine — Generate, adapt, and personalize your training programs based on your history, goals, and recovery data
  • Operate the AI Coach — Provide contextual coaching responses grounded in your actual training data
  • Improve the Service — Analyze aggregate, anonymized usage patterns to improve program quality, UI, and AI accuracy
  • Send transactional communications — Account confirmations, password resets, subscription receipts, and critical service updates
  • Provide customer support — Respond to your inquiries and resolve issues
  • Research — With your explicit consent, contribute your anonymized data to internal research improving hypertrophy science models

We do not use your personal training data to train models for third parties, sell insights to advertisers, or build behavioral advertising profiles.

Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party vendors who process data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements. These include cloud infrastructure (AWS), payment processing (Stripe), analytics (Posthog, anonymized), email delivery (Postmark), and error monitoring (Sentry).

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or if we have a good faith belief that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

Business Transfers

If RepForge AI is acquired or merged, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via email and in-app notification before any transfer occurs and give you the option to delete your account.

With Your Consent

We may share data for other purposes with your explicit prior consent.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we permanently delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain it for longer (e.g., financial records for tax purposes — retained for 7 years in accordance with applicable law, but this applies only to transaction records, not training data).

Anonymized, aggregated data derived from your sessions (which cannot be linked back to you) may be retained indefinitely for research and product improvement purposes.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability — Export your training data in JSON or CSV format from Account Settings → Export Data
  • Objection — Object to certain processing activities
  • Restriction — Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • Withdrawal of consent — Withdraw consent for processing activities that rely on consent

To exercise any of these rights, go to Account Settings → Privacy or email us at privacy@repforge.ai. We will respond within 30 days.

EU/EEA residents have additional rights under the GDPR. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA. We honor these rights regardless of your location.

Security

We take security seriously and implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3
  • All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256
  • Access to production systems is restricted and logged
  • We conduct regular penetration tests and security audits
  • We offer two-factor authentication (2FA) for all accounts
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with per-user salts

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Essential cookies — Authentication, session management, security (cannot be disabled)
  • Analytics cookies — Anonymized usage statistics (opt-out available)
  • Preference cookies — Remembering your settings and preferences

We do not use advertising cookies or allow third-party advertising networks to track you through our Service. You can manage cookie preferences from the Cookie Settings link in our footer.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at privacy@repforge.ai and we will take steps to delete such information.

Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you by email and in-app notification at least 14 days before the changes take effect. Your continued use of the Service after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

We maintain a version history of this policy available on request.
